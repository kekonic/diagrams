Revive sequence diagram presentation. Execution bars nest, arrows meet those bars, and sync, async, reply, and failure messages use distinct heads. Labels sit on chips with autonumber badges. Combined fragments hug the participants, notes, and labels they cover, with an operator tab and a guard that stays clear of execution bars. Notes wrap, dividers break for their label, `create` drops a participant onto the timeline, and `destroy` ends the lifeline. Longer sequences repeat participant names along the bottom.
